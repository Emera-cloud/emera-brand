## Brand Personality

Emera sits at the intersection of editorial sophistication and strategic precision. The brand is:

- **Intentional** — every element earns its place. Nothing is decorative without purpose.
- **Direct** — we lead with the point. No filler, no fluff.
- **Confident** — we make bold statements and back them with evidence.
- **Intelligent** — we communicate complexity simply, without dumbing it down.
- **Warm but not casual** — approachable expertise, not performative friendliness.

The brand voice pairs the gravitas of a seasoned strategist with the clarity of a great communicator. We speak the way a trusted advisor would — knowledgeable, grounded, and without ego.

| **Emera Sub** | `#5a5a5a` | Subdued text, captions, metadata, secondary labels. |

### Accent Color

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Emera Accent** | `#f0f191` | Highlight yellow-green. Use sparingly — tags, labels, decorative dividers, hover states. Never as a background for large areas. |

### Usage Rules

- **Dark on light or light on dark.** The palette is built for high contrast. Avoid mid-tone combinations.
- The accent (`#f0f191`) is a punctuation mark, not a backdrop. Use it for small, purposeful moments.
- The off-white (`#f7f7f5`) is the preferred page background — not pure white.
- Dark sections use `#0f0f0f` as the background with white text and the accent for highlights.

## Typography

### Typefaces

| Role | Font | Style | Stack |
|------|------|-------|-------|
| **Heading** | Cormorant Garant | Serif | `"Cormorant Garant", Georgia, serif` |
| **Body:** DM Sans | Sans-serif | `"DM Sans", system-ui, sans-serif` |

### Cormorant Garant (Headings)

A refined, editorial serif with elegant proportions. Use for all display text, hero headlines, section titles, and pull quotes. Set generously — large sizes let the letterforms breathe.

- **Weights used:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700)
- **Tone:** Sophisticated, timeless, premium
- **Best at:** Large sizes (32px and above). Avoid for body text or anything below 18px.

### DM Sans (Body)

A geometric sans-serif designed for readability at all sizes. Clean, modern, and unpretentious. Used for all body copy, UI text, labels, navigation, and supporting content.

- **Weights used:** Regular (400), Medium (500)
- **Tone:** Clear, accessible, contemporary
- **Best at:** Body copy (16–18px), UI elements, captions, meta text

### Type Scale

| Element | Font | Weight | Size (Desktop) |
|---------|------|--------|----------------|
| Hero / Display | Cormorant Garant | Bold | 80–120px |
| H1 | Cormorant Garant | Bold | 56–72px |
| H2 | Cormorant Garant | Medium | 40–48px |
| H3 | Cormorant Garant | Medium | 28–36px |
| Lead / Intro | DM Sans | Regular | 20–22px |
| Body | DM Sans | Regular | 16–18px |
| Caption / Label | DM Sans | Medium | 12–14px |
| Nav / UI | DM Sans | Medium | 14–16px |

### Typography Rules

- Headlines are set in Cormorant Garant with generous line height (1.1–1.2 for display, 1.3 for H1–H2).
- Body copy uses DM Sans at 1.6-–1.7 line height for readability.
- Labels and section tags are set in DM Sans, uppercase, with tracked letter spacing (0.08–0.12em).
- Never use italic Cormorant for body text. Reserve italics for editorial emphasis in headlines only.

## Layout & Grid

### Grid System

- **Desktop:** 12-column grid, 80px gutters on outer edges, 24px column gaps
- **Tablet:** 8-column grid, 48px outer gutters
- **Mobile:** 4-column grid, 24px outer gutters

### Spacing Scale

Base unit: 8px. All spacing values are multiples of 8.

`8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 80 · 96 · 128`

### Layout Character

- Pages use a subtle grid overlay (hairline lines) as a background texture — adds structure without visual noise.
- Sections alternate between light (`#f7f7f5`) and dark (`#0f0f0f`) backgrounds for visual rhythm.
- Content is left-aligned in hero sections with generous white space.
- Stats and metrics appear in large, bold DM Sans numerals paired with short descriptors.

## UI Components

### Buttons

**Primary (Filled Dark)**
- Background: `#0f0f0f`
- Text: `#ffffff`, DM Sans Medium, 14–16px
- Padding: 14px 28px
- Border-radius: 4px (nearly square — not pill-shaped)
- Use for: Primary CTAs ("Start a conversation", "Let's Talk")

**Secondary (Outlined)**
- Background: transparent
- Border: 1.5px solid `#0f0f0f`
- Text: `#0f0f0f`, DM Sans Medium, 14–16px
- Padding: 14px 28px
- Border-radius: 4px
- Use for: Secondary actions ("See what we build")

**Accent Tag / Badge**
- Background: `#f0f191`
- Text: `#0f0f0f`, DM Sans Medium, 12px uppercase, tracked
- Padding: 6px 14px
- Border-radius: 100px (pill)
- Use for: Category labels, service tags ("AI BRAND & GROWTH STUDIO")

### Cards

- Background: `#f7f7f5` or `#ffffff`
- Border: 1px solid `#e0e0dc`
- Padding: 32–48px
- No drop shadows — borders and background differentiation create hierarchy
- Corner radius: 4–8px

### Dividers

- Use thin hairline rules (`#e0e0dc`, 1px) to separate sections within a surface
- The accent color (`#f0f191`) is used as a decorative short rule/dash (not full-width) to mark section transitions

### Navigation

- Logo: EMERA wordmark in uppercase, Cormorant Garant Bold, `#0f0f0f`
- Nav links: DM Sans Regular, `#0f0f0f`, 15px
- CTA button: Primary filled dark style
- Background: white or transparent, borderless — clean and unobtrusive

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Principles

**1. Lead with the insight, not the setup.**
Don't build to the point. Start with it. "Great brands don't grow by accident" — not "Many businesses struggle to grow their brand consistently."

**2. Be specific. Avoid vague superlatives.**
"3× faster campaign delivery" beats "significantly faster results."

**3. Use active, confident constructions.**
"We build. We automate. We grow." — not "Our team is able to help you build and automate..."

**4. Speak in systems, not services.**
We don't "offer social media management." We "build content systems that keep your marketing always on."

**5. Human intelligence, not AI hype.**
Reference AI as a capability, not a gimmick. "AI-powered execution" not "cutting-edge artificial intelligence technology."

### Tone Modifiers by Context

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Website headlines | Bold, declarative, editorial |
| Proposals / documents | Strategic, considered, authoritative |
| Email | Direct, warm, efficient |
| Social media | Sharp, insightful, occasionally playful |
| Client-facing reports | Clear, evidence-backed, action-oriented |

### Words We Use

strategic · intentional · connected · intelligent · precise · systems · growth · architecture · execution · emerging · clarity

### Words We Avoid

synergy · revolutionary · game-changing · solutions provider · leverage (as a verb) · paradigm · cutting-edge · seamless (overused)

## Logo Usage

### Wordmark

The Emera logo is the EMERA wordmark set in Cormorant Garant Bold, uppercase, with standard tracking.

- **Preferred:** Black (`#0f0f0f`) wordmark on white or light backgrounds
- **Reversed:** White (`#ffffff`) wordmark on dark (`#0f0f0f`) backgrounds
- **Minimum size:** 80px wide (digital), 20mm (print)
- **Clear space:** Minimum 1× the cap height on all sides

### Rules

- Do not alter the typeface, tracking, or weight of the wordmark.
- Do not add a tagline to the wordmark in lockup.
- Do not use the accent color (`#f0f191`) for the wordmark itself.
- Do not place the wordmark on the accent yellow background.

## Imagery & Visual Style

### Photography

- Prefer clean, purposeful images with editorial restraint
- Avoid stock-feel photography — real environments, real work, real people
- High contrast, desaturated-to-neutral tones preferred over vibrant color photography
- Black and white is appropriate and on-brand for thought leadership contexts

### Illustration / Graphic Elements

- Grid overlays and hairline rules are signature texture elements
- Data visualizations and stats should use the type system (DM Sans numerals) and brand colors only
- Avoid decorative gradients — use solid color blocks

## Document & Collateral Standards

### Page Layouts

**Proposals & Reports**
- Cover: Dark background (`#0f0f0f`), EMERA wordmark in white, document title in Cormorant Garant, white
- Interior: White or off-white (`#f7f7f5`) backgrounds
- Section headers: Cormorant Garant Bold, `#0f0f0f`
- Body text: DM Sans Regular, `#1a1a1a`, 11–12pt
- Accent dividers: `#f0f191` short rule before section titles
- Page numbers: DM Sans, `#5a5a5a`, small, bottom center or bottom right

**Presentations / Slide Decks**
- Title slides: Dark background, large Cormorant Garant headline, white text
- Content slides: Off-white background, black text
- Data/stats slides: Large DM Sans numerals, `#0f0f0f`, with short descriptors in `#5a5a5a`
- Accent color sparingly for highlighted terms, labels, or active nav indicators

**One-Pagers**
- Single-column or two-column layouts
- Strong headline hierarchy using the type scale
- Logo top-left, contact bottom-right
- CTA box using dark background block with white text

### File Naming Convention

`YYYY-MM-DD_DocumentType_ClientOrProject_v01.ext`

Example: `2026-06-09_Proposal_ClientName_v01.pdf`
